Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Download Quizza from the App Store, open the app, and tap "Sign Up". You can register with your email address or use Sign in with Apple for a quick setup.

How does the ELO ranking system work?

Our ELO system calculates your skill rating based on match outcomes. Beating higher-ranked players gives more points, while losing to lower-ranked players costs more. Everyone starts at 1000 ELO, and your rank adjusts after each match.

Can I play offline?

Multiplayer matches require an internet connection. We're working on a solo practice mode that will work offline in a future update.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Please email us at hello@diggolabs.tech with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful!

How can I report inappropriate behavior?

We take user safety seriously. To report a player, tap their profile after a match and select "Report User". You can also email us at hello@diggolabs.tech with details.

Are there in-app purchases?

Quizza is free to play. We may offer optional cosmetic items in the future, but gameplay will always remain free and fair for everyone.

Which devices are supported?

Quizza requires iOS 15.0 or later and works on iPhone. iPad support is coming soon.
