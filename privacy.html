Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 25, 2026

This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: email, username, and authentication identifiers.
  • Gameplay data: scores, match history, rankings, and category ratings.
  • Device data: device type, OS version, and basic diagnostics.
  • Support messages: details you send to us for help.

2. How We Use Data

  • Provide gameplay, matchmaking, and leaderboards.
  • Maintain account security and prevent abuse.
  • Process purchases and subscriptions through Apple.
  • Improve app stability and performance.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data with service providers to run the app, such as:

  • Authentication and storage providers
  • Apple services for Sign in with Apple and in‑app purchases

4. Advertising and Analytics

We may introduce advertising or analytics providers in the future to support and improve the app. If we do, we will update this policy with the specific providers and the data they process.

5. Data Retention

We retain data while your account is active and only as long as needed for legitimate business or legal purposes.

6. Your Choices

  • You can update or delete your account in the app.
  • You can manage subscription settings in your App Store account.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards to protect your data. No system is 100% secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

Quizza is not intended for children under 13. If we learn we collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date when changes are made.

10. Contact

Questions? Email hello@diggolabs.tech.
